Export your data

Download JSON containing your account profile, plants including archived plants, tags, care schedules/history, notification preferences and calendar/device metadata. Passwords, tokens and device encryption keys are excluded. This is a data copy, not an automatic restore file.

Permanently delete your account

Deletion removes your account and its plants, care history, notifications, device subscriptions and calendar access from the active service. You will be signed out. This action cannot be undone in the app.

Existing encrypted backups may still contain earlier data and become eligible for removal after 30 days. Cleanup may be delayed if backup storage is unavailable or verification fails. A minimal deletion marker is retained to prevent your account returning during managed recovery. External calendar copies, downloaded exports and messages already delivered are not erased by this action. A reminder already in delivery may still arrive.

If an external calendar is connected, disconnect it on Calendar first. Archiving a plant is different from account deletion and preserves its records.
