## Product and experience

- **Dashboard and reminders.** Make the first session easy to finish. Lead from choosing a plant to adding it, setting a reminder and completing care. Show the next action and the reason it is due. Test real installed-device action icons, snoozed follow-ups, notification deduplication and Dashboard navigation. Keep in-app due care reliable when push is unavailable or denied.
- **My plants and care history.** Add server-side history filters and cursor paging for people who exceed the current 100-entry display. Add bulk care completion only if testers need it, with clear per-plant confirmation and an idempotency test. For offline, first consider a narrowly scoped read-only view. Offline writes deserve a separate design for retries, duplicate actions, conflicts, logout and account deletion.
- **Calendar and sharing.** Complete a real calendar-client feed test using a synthetic account. Verify updates, revoke and rotate the feed, and keep URLs out of logs and screenshots. Confirm Brevo invite, confirmation and reset delivery with an authorized test inbox. Keep the month grid compact and preserve the day agenda and direct links to plant details. Defer Google or Microsoft synchronization until demand justifies its permissions and support cost.
- **Garden and Space planner.** Finish the mobile flow with a zone-to-pot smoke test, visible selection and keyboard/screen-reader feedback. Add plant placement hints with a clear explanation of their evidence. For a larger feature, design account-synced layouts with a one-time opt-in import from the browser, a per-layout revision check, and export/deletion coverage. Layouts currently live in browser storage, which limits cross-device use and recovery. Offer clearly marked starter layouts for a windowsill, balcony and small garden after testing blank-layout onboarding.
- **Catalog and images.** Grow by category gaps and by what users search for, with common English names first. Keep exact cultivar photo provenance and pending labels. Prioritize the 117 pending cultivars with the most browsing demand and add more verified growth-stage examples. The public API currently returns the whole catalog. Measure download size, rendering and search on a mid-range phone, then add bounded pages or progressive rendering if needed. Keep deep links, filters and comparison selections stable across the change.
- **Photo journal.** Keep activation a separate decision. The private upload path and isolated decoder have useful local evidence, but the [host preflight](photo-candidate-preflight.md) and [mixed-load rehearsal](photo-mixed-workload.md) are separate from a running OVH gateway. Before enabling, accept real host installation, restart and failure handling, input limits, upload/export quotas, deletion-aware recovery, dependency notices and headroom under normal use. If enabled, make photo progress a private timeline. Consider plant diagnosis only later, with clear uncertainty and a separate privacy review.
- **Navigation and accessibility.** Keep the current top-level navigation. A new tab should earn its place through repeated use. Run touch, keyboard, focus, text zoom, contrast and screen-reader acceptance across sign-up, Dashboard, catalog, calendar, planner, account and any ad surface. Add a narrow-phone and landscape browser case for the planner. Retain physical device checks alongside automated ones.

## Security and release engineering

- **Proxy trust and perimeter.** Compose enables forwarded headers, and `PlantCare.Api/Program.cs` clears the trusted proxy lists in that mode. The intended boundary is the internal Caddy-to-API path. Verify the API and SQL are inaccessible from the internet over IPv4 and IPv6. Then restrict forwarded headers to the trusted proxy/network and test spoofed headers, client-IP rate limits and HTTPS cookies through Caddy. Verify current SSH access and OVH console recovery before changing firewall or SSH rules.
- **Private responses and token redaction.** The PWA excludes API navigation, and photos, shortlist and account endpoints already have cache directives in relevant paths. Extend explicit `Cache-Control: no-store` to sensitive reads where needed. Test a signed-in collection response through Caddy, sign-out, account switch, and any future edge cache. Redact private feed URLs, reset parameters, cookies, authorization headers, email addresses and photo metadata from server, reverse-proxy and client diagnostics.
- **Abuse and ownership.** Preserve the existing auth and resource ownership tests. Add tests for cross-account planner access when sync arrives, invitation spam, repeated password-reset requests, and large payloads. Check where an IP-based limit should be complemented with account or destination quotas, especially on shared networks. Protect operator accounts with MFA and documented recovery access.
- **Dependencies and artifacts.** Current CI covers functional, isolation and recovery scenarios, with a separate catalog path. Add dependency and container vulnerability checks, a shipped-image inventory, pinned runtime digests, and a patch cadence. Use a narrow scheduled security job and run the broader suites only for meaningful candidate batches or sensitive changes. Finish backend/native notices and verify the shipped artifacts. Keep release evidence tied to the exact commit and image digest.

## OVH operating plan

Keep the current 4 GB OVH VPS for staging and a limited public beta if measured capacity supports it. Continue building images off-host, keep SQL private, and deploy pinned artifacts. A second VPS, a database migration or Kubernetes should follow a demonstrated capacity or recovery need.

- Track free disk, SQL data and log growth, backup age, memory pressure and OOM restarts, request latency, and the oldest pending worker item. Keep UptimeRobot readiness and operations checks, then add an independent backup freshness check using a minimal success signal. Agree on thresholds from the baseline, including reserve for logs, SQL backups and one rollback image.
- Retain encrypted external-drive backups. The current VPS capture depends on the owner's Windows session, drive, SSH agent and network. For public launch, design an unattended encrypted off-server copy with a separate recovery key and an alert outside the VPS. Propose a starting target of at most 24 hours of lost writes and four hours to restore, then measure what can actually be met. Rehearse restoration with a fresh Linux environment, keys and database, outbound email/push isolated, and deletion reconciliation before serving users. [Backup runbook](vps-backups.md) · [Erasure-safe recovery](retention-and-erasure-recovery.md)
- Keep provider snapshots as an additional recovery path. Record the OVH backup window, observe readiness and worker recovery around it, and keep the support escalation steps for a stuck task. Schedule application deployments away from that window where possible. Protect application recovery with encrypted SQL and key backups regardless of the provider snapshot state. [OVH automated backup guide](https://docs.ovhcloud.com/en/guides/bare-metal-cloud/virtual-private-servers/using-automated-backups-on-a-vps)
- Compose specifies SQL Server Express. Measure current database and media growth before increasing photo quotas. Treat the documented Express database size and resource limits as design constraints. A host RAM upgrade alone does not remove an edition limit. Use the current setup until the data shows that a different storage or database strategy is needed. [Microsoft SQL Server 2022 editions on Linux](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sql/linux/sql-server-linux-editions-and-components-2022)

## App Store, Google Play, and ads

**Apple first-class experience.** Keep the shared Angular UI and evaluate a Capacitor-based native build with the planner, camera or photo picker, native push and deep links as app-specific workflows. Test session/CSRF behavior before selecting the bridge. Keep cookies or platform-protected credentials and a narrow origin policy. A Home Screen PWA and an App Store binary have separate acceptance paths. Get access to macOS/Xcode and a physical iPhone and iPad before signing off. Apple reviews minimum functionality, requires in-app initiation of account deletion for apps with account creation, and expects reviewer access and accurate privacy disclosures. Provide a dedicated review account and walkthrough for zone planning and care. [Apple review guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) · [Account deletion](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/) · [App Privacy details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/)

**Google Play.** Compare a Trusted Web Activity with a shared native shell. A TWA fits the PWA path. A native shell may make native ads and push easier to share across platforms. Decide after testing sign-in, app updates, notifications and ad consent on a physical Android device. Prepare Data safety disclosures, a deletion URL and test-track access. Google currently requires qualifying new personal accounts to complete a closed test with at least 12 continuously opted-in testers for 14 days before applying for production access. [Android TWA](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/layout/webapps/trusted-web-activities) · [Google Play closed testing](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465) · [Google Play account deletion](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111)

**Publication decisions.** Keep the deferred public contact-address decision in the launch checklist. Review the operator notice, support and privacy URLs, backup/erasure policy, providers and data transfers, and the EU trader declaration before submission. Ads make the commercial-use plan relevant to that review. Confirm which contact details Apple would display and decide the publication address with the owner. Keep staging as a test environment while closing these gates. [Apple EU trader requirements](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/manage-european-union-digital-services-act-trader-requirements/) · [EDPB small-business data protection guide](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/process-personal-data-lawfully_en)

**Ads with minimal data.** My recommendation is an ad-free private beta and a later ad-supported release. Start with a single clearly labeled contextual banner or inline card on the public catalog. Keep ads away from care completion, account and privacy screens, notifications and widgets. Do not pass personal plant names, location, photos, account IDs, care history or sharing data to the SDK. If using AdMob, follow its current consent/CMP rules for the target regions and ad modes, use test ads first, and configure app-ads.txt if required for the selected setup. Check the consent state before requesting ads, offer withdrawal, and check whether any data flows require Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission. Complete the store disclosures and SDK privacy manifests for the actual shipped SDKs. Keep a kill switch and measure aggregate impressions, net revenue, crashes, and retention before adding placements. [Apple privacy and tracking](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/user-privacy-and-data-use/) · [Apple SDK requirements](https://developer.apple.com/support/third-party-SDK-requirements/) · [AdMob consent platform rules](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/13554116) · [AdMob consent SDK](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy)

**Measure whether ads pay for their cost.** Track aggregate impressions, net publisher revenue per thousand impressions, and the change in care completion and return visits after launch. For a rough break-even estimate, divide monthly incremental costs by the observed net revenue per thousand impressions and multiply by 1,000. Use measured results to decide whether to keep the placement or expand it. Include store enrollment and build costs when comparing total project spend with revenue.
